# Mapsmith Privacy Policy

Effective November 10, 2026. This policy covers Mapsmith for Mac.

**Mapsmith collects no data.** It has no account, no analytics, no advertising, no tracking and no third-party
code that reports anything. Nothing you do in the app is sent to the developer or anyone else. Mapsmith makes no
network requests at all.

## What stays on your Mac

- **The maps you open** (PDFs and images) are read on your Mac to find the grid and cut the map into pages. They
  are never uploaded. Mapsmith saves nothing of them except the tile PDFs you export, where you choose to put them.
- **Recent files** are remembered on your Mac so Open Recent works. Clear Menu forgets them.

Deleting the app from your Mac leaves this behind: macOS keeps an app's saved data in the Containers folder of
your Library folder until you delete the folder named Mapsmith there.

## Other apps on your Mac

Mapsmith can ask Scrollkeeper, a separate app, for the list of maps you own and to download one. Those requests go
from app to app on your Mac, through a folder the two share; Scrollkeeper's own policy covers what it does with
them. Mapsmith can also receive images from Pluck the same way.

## Printing

Printing goes through macOS to the printer you choose.

## Crash reports

If you choose to share analytics with app developers in your Mac's settings, Apple may send crash reports for
Mapsmith to the developer. These are governed by Apple's privacy policy and contain no personal data from the app.

## Children

Mapsmith collects no data from anyone, including children.

## Changes and contact

Changes to this policy will be posted here, with a new effective date. Questions go to the
